Trick settings

Tokens: MM month, DD day, YY / YYYY year, hh 12-hour, HH 24-hour, mm minutes. Example: MMDDhhmm → 11031435 style.
After this many numbers have gone in, the next number typed after a + gets forced.
Add minutes if the reveal happens a while after the last number goes in. Times past :40 seconds round up automatically.
